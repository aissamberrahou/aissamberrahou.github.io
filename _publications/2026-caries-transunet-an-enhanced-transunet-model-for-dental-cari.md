This study aims to develop an enhanced version of the TransUNet architecture to improve the segmentation accuracy across various caries types in panoramic dental X-rays. The DC1000 dental caries dataset was used for this study. We propose Caries-TransUNet, an enhanced version of TransUNet, designed to improve the identification and segmentation of diverse caries types in dental panoramic radiography. The proposed model’s performance was compared against several state-of-the-art models, including U-Net, Attention UNet, DeepLabv3+ and TransUNet, using standard metrics such as Accuracy, Dice score, Intersection over Union (IoU), Precision and Recall. The proposed model attained an Accuracy of 99.81%, Dice score of 77.88%, IoU of 63.85%, Precision at 81.37% and Recall reaching 76.48% surpassing TransUNet and other state-of-the-art models consistently in Dice score, IoU score and Recall while demonstrating robust segmentation of all three types of caries across multiple X-ray images.
