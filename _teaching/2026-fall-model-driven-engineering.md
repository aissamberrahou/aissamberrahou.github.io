Model-driven engineering (IDM, *Ingénierie Dirigée par les Modèles*) puts models at the heart of software development. This course covers the OMG's Model-Driven Architecture (MDA): metamodelling, UML profiles, model-to-model and model-to-text transformations, and domain-specific languages, using the Eclipse Modeling tools.

## Prerequisites
- UML (class, sequence and state diagrams)
- Object-oriented programming (Java)

## Tools
- [Eclipse Modeling Tools](https://www.eclipse.org/downloads/packages/) with EMF, ATL, Acceleo and Sirius

## Project
In groups, students define a small domain-specific language, with its metamodel, editor, transformations and code generator, for a domain of their choice.
