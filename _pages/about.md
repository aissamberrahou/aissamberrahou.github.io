I am an Associate Professor at the National School of Computer Science and Systems Analysis (ENSIAS), Mohammed V University in Rabat, Morocco. My teaching and research activities focus on Embedded Systems, Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Edge AI, and Intelligent Industrial Systems.

My teaching covers several areas, including Embedded Systems, Microcontroller Programming, Data Structures, Object-Oriented Programming, Model-Driven Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence, with a strong emphasis on combining theoretical foundations with practical, hands-on laboratory activities.

My research interests focus on the design and development of intelligent embedded and industrial systems, particularly at the intersection of Embedded Systems, Internet of Things (IoT), Edge AI, Industrial AI, Cyber-Physical Systems, and Cybersecurity.

I am particularly interested in developing intelligent, connected, reliable, and secure systems for industrial and real-world applications.
